In 1637, Pierre de Fermat scribbled in the margin of Diophantus' Arithmetica that he possessed a truly marvelous proof, but the margin was too small to contain it. For 358 years, humanity was forced to rely on blind trust. MARGINALIA inverts this ancient limitation. We construct a cryptographic margin capable of holding the proof in zero knowledge. You do not trust assertions. You verify mathematical invariants on-chain.
